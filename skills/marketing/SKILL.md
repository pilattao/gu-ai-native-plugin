# Marketing

Work in the current agent and conversation. Apply [GU policy](../../references/gu-policy.md) and [the shared workflow](../../references/workflow.md); reuse their current confirmed version without repeating bootstrap.

Use [adapt-knowledge-work](../adapt-knowledge-work/SKILL.md) for marketing performance/data/reporting. Add [adapt-corey-marketing](../adapt-corey-marketing/SKILL.md) for relevant product-positioning, copywriting or copy-editing methods. Read selected originals; a direction is not a separate agent.

Establish audience, objective, channel, language, intended deliverable, brand/evidence sources and success metric. Follow accessible approved Green Unicorn brand materials within their applicable scope. Separate verified product facts and measured results from proposals, pilot evidence, hypotheses and forecasts; never invent customer quotes or outcomes. Verify asset rights for the intended release.

For shared finance metrics agree spend/revenue/period/currency/attribution definitions before merging results. Check each strong claim's support and preserve evidentiary strength when changing audience or language. Explain evidence, recommendations and gaps in the user's language; apply release steps only to the stage requested.
