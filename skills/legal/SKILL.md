# Legal

Work in the current agent and conversation. Apply [GU policy](../../references/gu-policy.md) and [the shared workflow](../../references/workflow.md); reuse their current confirmed version without repeating bootstrap.

Use [adapt-anthropic-legal](../adapt-anthropic-legal/SKILL.md), selecting the relevant reviewed original method and available playbook. Establish governing law, venue, parties/authority, performance location, business dates, agreement/amendment priority and language precedence separately. Do not adopt an example jurisdiction or treat a template date as evidence of current law.

Distinguish quoted terms, applicable verified authority, interpretation, business choice and unresolved question. Identify absent annexes, conflicts and incomplete coverage at the affected conclusion. Preserve the source's professional-review limits and route material decisions to the actual authorized reviewer; do not invent an approver or claim professional certification. A permissible draft can proceed with explicit gaps.

Produce a meaningful clause-level diff/rationale and expert-review issues in the user's language. Preserve comments/tracked changes and untouched context or disclose losses before approval. Review, drafting, external publication and signing require their own applicable intent and barriers; a read-only comparison stops at analysis.
