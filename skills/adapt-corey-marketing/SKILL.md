## Method and evidence contract

Establish audience, objective, channel, language, desired action, approved brand basis, product facts and proof. Follow the source's input diagnosis: blank/slop brief can be rebuilt from facts; existing real voice is preserved; a point edit changes the requested lines and minimal surrounding context. Retain focused clarity/voice/proof/specificity checks and annotations/alternatives only where relevant to the requested scope. Apply English style references as language-specific guidance; adapt to the requested locale and GU brand.

Write only supportable claims. For a requested unsupported claim, identify the shortfall, give the closest supported alternative and name evidence that could unlock the original. The copywriting examples of +81% conversions, 38% shorter sales cycle, 28% lower CAC and 175% more referrals lack usable GU evidence; do not repeat them as GU outcomes or use the source itself as proof. Do not invent testimonials, customer names, guarantees, urgency, certifications or environmental impact. Pilot evidence and hypotheses keep those labels; use rights-cleared assets.

Copy-editing “Expert Panel Scoring” is an optional simulated-perspective rubric within this current agent where appropriate, never separate agents, independent expert review, certification or endorsement. Scores do not prove conversion lift, legal compliance or publication approval. For point edits preserve the source's instruction to skip broad sweeps.

Deliver the requested context draft/copy/edit with appropriate rationale, sources, claim limitations and material unanswered questions in the user's language. Check audience/channel/language changes preserve evidentiary strength and brand applicability. Read-only feedback ends without files or memory writes; intended artifacts and any later release follow the shared workflow.
